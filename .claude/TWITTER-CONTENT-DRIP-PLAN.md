## Posting Rules

2. **Always include image** (header from blog or quote card)
3. **Link to full content** (blog or Bluesky)
4. **Never duplicate content** (unique hooks each time)
5. **Mix topics** (don't cluster all "infrastructure" together)
6. **Engage with replies** (counts against quota, budget 5/day)
