## Research Findings

### Paper Verified (arXiv:2506.10943)
- **Title**: Self-Adapting Language Models
- **Authors**: Adam Zweiger, Jyothish Pari, Han Guo, Ekin Akyurek, Yoon Kim, Pulkit Agrawal
- **Institution**: MIT (Improbable AI Lab)
- **Presented**: NeurIPS 2025

### OpenAI Connection - CONFIRMED
Ekin Akyurek, co-author of SEAL paper, is now at OpenAI as "Member of Technical Staff" working on Codex. This validates the claim that SEAL researchers are at OpenAI.

### Key SEAL Capabilities
1. Models generate their own finetuning data
2. Self-edits produce persistent weight updates
3. Learns from new data without external retraining
4. Outperformed GPT-4.1 synthetic data despite being smaller model

### Important Limitation
Paper notes "catastrophic forgetting" problem - repeated self-edits degrade performance on earlier tasks. Technology promising but not solved.

### GPT-6 Status
- No official announcement yet (as of 2026-01-08)
- Sam Altman says GPT-6 will focus on "memory, agentic features"
- Expected release: first half 2026
- SEAL technology aligns with stated GPT-6 goals

## Lesson Learned

Social media speculation often has a kernel of truth worth verifying. The "SEAL researchers at OpenAI" claim was accurate (Ekin Akyurek). The GPT-6 integration is reasonable speculation aligned with Altman's public statements, not confirmed fact.
