## Research Brief 2: MCP Technical Deep-Dive (web-researcher)

### What MCP Is
"USB-C for AI" - universal connector for AI-to-tool integration

**Protocol**: JSON-RPC 2.0 over stdio (local) or HTTP/WebSocket (remote)

**Three Primitives**:
- **Resources**: Read-only data (databases, files, docs)
- **Tools**: Executable functions (run code, send emails, query APIs)
- **Prompts**: Templated workflows

### Adoption Numbers (Dec 2025)
- 97 million monthly SDK downloads
- 10,000+ public MCP servers
- OpenAI adopted March 2025
- Google, Microsoft, Amazon all founding members
- Linux Foundation now governs via Agentic AI Foundation

### CEO vs Employee Concrete Example

**Employee (Vanilla ChatGPT)**:
- "What were our Q4 sales figures?"
- ChatGPT: "I don't have access to your company data..."

**CEO with MCP**:
- PostgreSQL + Salesforce + Slack MCP servers connected
- Same question yields: "Q4 sales were $4.2M, up 23% from Q3. Top performer: Sarah (12 deals)..."

### Security Considerations (April 2025 research)
- Prompt injection through tool responses
- Tool permission escalation
- Agentic AI Foundation making security 2026 priority
